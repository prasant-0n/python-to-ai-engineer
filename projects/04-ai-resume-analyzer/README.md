# Project 04 — AI Resume Analyzer

**Focus:** structured document extraction and evidence-based analysis.

## Requirements
- Define a clear input/output schema and transparent scoring rubric.
- Distinguish extracted evidence from model-generated interpretation.
- Validate outputs and handle malformed or oversized files safely.
- Protect personal data; do not retain uploads unnecessarily.
- Evaluate on representative examples and document bias/limitations.

## Completion evidence
Results are structured, traceable to evidence, evaluated, and accompanied by privacy and limitation notes.

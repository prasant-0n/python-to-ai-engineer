# Project 09 — Multi-tenant AI SaaS

**Focus:** production-minded AI application architecture.

## Requirements
- Enforce tenant isolation at every data and retrieval boundary.
- Implement authentication, authorization, quotas, and rate limits.
- Track usage, model/provider costs, latency, and failures.
- Provide health checks, structured logs, tests, and deployment automation.
- Document threat model, retention policy, incident response, and recovery.

## Completion evidence
Demonstrate isolation tests, usage controls, operational dashboards or reports, and a reproducible deployment.
